# Building EDITFLOW AI: A Solo Builder's Journey

Hi everyone!

I'm building **EDITFLOW AI**, a story-aware editing tool for creators who make faceless documentaries, mystery videos, history content, and narrated stories.

The idea comes from a problem I keep thinking about: making a compelling video is not just about generating or collecting clips. The editor also needs to understand how the story fits together, keep scenes connected to the narration, organize assets, and make it easy to move from an idea to an editable timeline.

## What I'm trying to build

My current direction for EDITFLOW AI includes:

- Organizing projects, media assets, and editing timelines.
- Keeping a reliable history of timeline revisions.
- Connecting story elements and evidence to relevant moments in the video.
- Exploring a Story Graph view alongside timestamp-linked video playback.
- Rendering videos and checking output integrity and quality.

The project is being developed incrementally. Some foundations are already in the repository, while other parts are still under development. **EDITFLOW AI is not production-ready yet**, and I want to be transparent about that.

## My pricing intention

I want EDITFLOW AI to be **free or very low-cost for independent creators**.

My current thinking is to keep the core workflow free, while considering optional paid features or usage-based charges for resource-intensive services (such as storage or rendering) if they become necessary to cover operating costs. The exact model is still being explored; I don't want affordability to become an afterthought.

## Why I'm sharing this

I'm building this independently with AI-assisted development, and I'm learning as I go. I'm sharing the process because I'd value thoughtful feedback from people who have experience with:

- Video-editing systems and media pipelines.
- AI-assisted creative tools.
- Storytelling workflows and documentary production.
- Product architecture, developer experience, and open-source communities.

If you work in one of these areas, I'd appreciate your perspective. What would make a tool like this genuinely useful? What risks or technical challenges should I be thinking about early?

Thanks for taking a look. I'll share progress and lessons as the project develops.
